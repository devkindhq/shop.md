## Overview

Fold Electronics sells certified refurbished consumer electronics across
laptops, smartphones, tablets, monitors, and audio equipment. All units
are graded A, B, or C, factory reset, and tested to a 90-point checklist
before listing. Brands include Apple, Dell, Lenovo, Samsung, and Sony.
New accessories are stocked alongside refurbished hardware.

## Who It's For

Individual buyers and small business purchasers looking for verified
refurbished hardware at 30-60% below new retail. B2B purchasing is
available for orders of 5 or more units with volume pricing and a
dedicated account manager. All inventory is owned, graded, and shipped
by Fold directly, not a marketplace.

## Why Buy Here

In operation since 2016. 4.6 rating from 14,200 reviews on Trustpilot.
All units carry a 12-month warranty with next-business-day replacement on
failure. Grading definitions are published and applied consistently: a
Grade A unit has no visible marks at 30cm. B2B customers assigned a
dedicated account manager for orders over USD 2,500.

## Commerce

Ships to the US, Canada, UK, Australia, and New Zealand from Louisville,
KY. Standard delivery 3-5 business days within the US, 7-14 days
internationally. Free shipping on US orders over USD 100; flat-rate
international shipping. Returns accepted within 30 days at buyer's cost.
Accepts Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Affirm for US
buyers. Full policies at /policies.md.
