## Returns

Returns are accepted within 30 days of the delivery date. Items must be
returned in original condition with all accessories, cables, and
documentation included.

Grade A units must show no additional marks or damage beyond the condition
described at time of purchase. Grade B and Grade C units are accepted in
any condition within the 30-day window.

To initiate a return, email returns@foldelectronics.com with your order
number, the item you wish to return, and a brief description of the reason.
No prepaid return label is provided. Return shipping is at the buyer's cost.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days
of Fold receiving and inspecting the returned item. If a returned unit
arrives with damage not present at time of dispatch, or with accessories
missing, the refund amount may be reduced to reflect the difference.

**Warranty returns (defective units)**
The 30-day return policy is separate from the 12-month warranty. Confirmed
hardware failures within the warranty period are handled as warranty claims,
not returns. See Terms of Sale for warranty terms.

## Shipping

Fold Electronics ships to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom,
Australia, and New Zealand from its Louisville, KY warehouse.

**United States**
Standard (3-5 business days, FedEx or UPS): USD 9.95.
Free standard shipping on orders over USD 100.
Express (1-2 business days, FedEx or UPS): USD 24.95.
No free threshold for express shipping.

**Canada**
Standard (7-10 business days, UPS International): USD 24.95.
No free shipping threshold.

**United Kingdom**
Standard (7-12 business days, FedEx International): USD 29.95.
No free shipping threshold.

**Australia and New Zealand**
Standard (10-14 business days, FedEx International): USD 34.95.
No free shipping threshold.

Orders placed before 3pm EST Monday to Friday are dispatched the same business day from our Louisville, KY warehouse. Orders placed after 3pm EST or on weekends and US public holidays are dispatched the next business day.
Tracking is provided for all orders. Tracking numbers are emailed when the
order is dispatched.

**Lithium battery notice**
Laptops, smartphones, and tablets contain lithium batteries. These items
ship ground-only within the United States (standard shipping applies).
International shipments of lithium battery devices are processed via
approved air freight routes in compliance with IATA regulations; no
restriction on international ordering applies.

Customers outside the US are responsible for any applicable customs duties
or import taxes. These vary by destination country and are assessed by the
destination country's customs authority.

## Privacy

**Data collected**
Fold Electronics collects name, email address, shipping address, and
billing address when an order is placed. Payment card data is processed
by Stripe; Fold does not store card numbers or CVVs. Browsing data is
collected via Shopify Analytics and Google Analytics (anonymised). For
B2B accounts, company name and VAT or tax identification number are also
collected.

**How it is used**
Order and address data is used to fulfil and ship orders, send order
confirmations and tracking notifications, and handle returns, warranty
claims, and customer support. B2B account data is used to administer
wholesale agreements and volume pricing. Email addresses are not added to
marketing communications without explicit opt-in.

**Third-party sharing**
Personal data is shared with:
- FedEx, UPS, and other carrier partners, for order delivery (name and
  shipping address only).
- Shopify Inc., as the platform operator, under Shopify's privacy policy
  (shopify.com/legal/privacy).
- Google LLC, for analytics (anonymised and aggregated data only; no
  personally identifiable information is shared).

Fold Electronics does not sell personal data to third parties.

**Retention**
Order records, including associated personal data, are retained for 7
years to comply with US federal tax record-keeping requirements. B2B
account and wholesale agreement records are retained for the same period.
Marketing list membership is retained until the shopper unsubscribes.

**Shopper rights**
Shoppers may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their
personal data at any time. To submit a request, email
privacy@foldelectronics.com with the subject line "Data Request" and the
email address associated with the account.

**Contact**
privacy@foldelectronics.com

## Terms of Sale

**Warranty**
All units carry a 12-month warranty from the date of delivery. On
confirmed hardware failure within the warranty period, Fold will dispatch
a replacement unit the next business day. The original unit must be
returned within 14 days of receiving the replacement, using a prepaid
label provided by Fold.

The warranty is void if the unit shows signs of liquid damage or
unauthorised repair. Physical damage caused after delivery is not covered
by the warranty but may be eligible for a return within the 30-day return
window.

**B2B and wholesale**
B2B orders of 5 or more units are governed by a separate wholesale
agreement. Volume pricing requires a signed order form. B2B customers with
orders over USD 2,500 are assigned a dedicated account manager. To open a
B2B account, contact wholesale@foldelectronics.com.
