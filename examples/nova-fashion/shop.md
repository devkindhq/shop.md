## Overview

Nova is a premium womenswear label focused on contemporary tailoring and
elevated casual wear. The range covers dresses, knitwear, outerwear, and
accessories across sizes XS to 3XL. Collections drop seasonally with
limited-run pieces; core styles remain in permanent stock year-round.

## Who It's For

Women aged 28-45 looking for elevated everyday wear and occasion pieces
that read as considered without being formal. Size range runs XS to 3XL
across all styles, not select pieces only. Price points sit at the entry
of premium, comparable to Theory or Vince rather than Prada or The Row.

## Why Buy Here

In operation since 2019. 4.7 rating from 6,800 reviews on Trustpilot.
Free worldwide returns within 60 days, no questions asked. Climate-neutral
shipping on all orders. Dedicated fit guide with model measurements and
size comparison tool. Alterations service available for AU and US customers.

## Commerce

Ships worldwide from Los Angeles. Standard delivery 2-4 business days
within the US, 5-8 days internationally. Free shipping on orders over
USD 150. Returns accepted within 60 days at no cost to the buyer. Accepts
Visa, Mastercard, Amex, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Klarna. Full policies at
