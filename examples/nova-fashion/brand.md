## Voice

Write in second person. Address as "you", not "shoppers" or "customers".

Short sentences, one idea each. Prefer under 18 words per sentence. Break any sentence that exceeds this into two.

Active voice throughout. "Nova ships worldwide" not "Worldwide shipping is available".

Never use: "luxurious", "elevated", "curated", "stunning", "gorgeous", "amazing", "incredible", "perfect", or "must-have".

Numbers under 10 written as words in editorial copy: "three styles", "two colourways". Numerals for measurements, prices, and product specifications: "38 cm sleeve length", "$220 USD", "size 10 (AU)".

No exclamation marks.

Do not open copy with a question. "Looking for the perfect dress?" is not the voice.

Avoid filler qualifiers: "really", "very", "quite", "just", "truly". If a word needs qualifying, rewrite the sentence.

## Tone

Baseline: quietly confident. Not cold, not warm. Precise. The copy assumes the reader is capable of making an informed decision. It gives her what she needs to make it.

**Product descriptions:** Lead with construction and fabric, then silhouette, then styling. The quality of the garment speaks. Copy does not argue for it. State what the piece is made of, how it fits, and what it is suited to. One styling suggestion is acceptable. More than one reads as pressure.

**Email (transactional):** Minimal. Order number, what was ordered, when it ships. No design copy in transactional email. No sign-off enthusiasm. "Your order is confirmed" not "We're so excited to get this to you".

**Email (marketing):** Editorial, not promotional. The angle is always "here is something that works well for a specific purpose", not "here is a sale". A new piece drops. The copy explains why Nova added it, what it is for, and who it will suit. Promotions, when they occur, lead with the product. The discount is secondary.

**Social:** Shorter sentences than editorial. One observation per post. No countdown timers. No "last few left" unless stock is confirmed at the time of posting. An image does more than copy here. Let it.

**Error messages:** Direct and actionable. "This item is out of stock in your size. Join the waitlist to be notified." Not "Oops! Looks like we're out." The brand does not use "Oops".

## Positioning

Nova occupies the considered-purchase segment of contemporary womenswear. The buyer has moved past trend-chasing. She wants pieces that work across contexts and seasons, fit consistently, and are made well enough to last. She is not buying a fantasy. She is buying something to wear.

Nova entered the market in 2019. The position it holds: quality-led contemporary womenswear at the entry of premium, sized XS to 3XL across all styles. The range is not exhaustive. It is deliberate. Collections drop seasonally. Core styles stay.

**What Nova would say:**

- "Cut from 100% merino wool sourced in New Zealand. The knit is mid-weight, suitable for three-season wear."
- "This runs close to size. If you prefer a relaxed fit, size up."
- "Available in three colourways. The navy is a true navy, darker than it reads on screen."
- "The sleeve length is 58 cm on a size 10 (AU). Full measurements are in the fit guide."

**What Nova would not say:**

- Claims about "timeless" pieces. The word is overused and unverifiable. A piece either holds up or it does not.
- Urgency fabrication: "selling fast", "almost gone", "only a few left" without confirmed stock data.
- Brand comparisons by name. Category language is acceptable.
- Sustainability claims without specific certification data. If a fabric has a certification, name it and name the certifying body. If it does not, do not claim it.
- "Investment piece", "wardrobe essential", "season staple". These phrases have been drained of meaning. Do not use them.

## Writing Rules

**Brand name:** Always "Nova" on first and subsequent mentions. Never "Nova Fashion" in copy. That is the domain name, not how the brand addresses itself.

**Product names:** Use exactly as they appear in the catalogue. Do not paraphrase or shorten.

**Sizing:** Use the size system of the shipping destination. "Size 10 (AU)" for AU customers. "Size 6 (US)" for US customers. Never abbreviate to just the number without the system context.

**Fabric and materials:** Always name the full composition: "100% merino wool", not "wool". Include country of origin when known: "100% merino wool, New Zealand".

**Currency:** USD prices as "$XX USD". AUD prices as "$XX AUD". GBP prices as "£XX GBP". EUR prices as "€XX EUR". Never "$XX" without the currency code.

**Dates:** Day-month-year: "7 July 2026". No ordinals. Not "7th July" or "July 7".

**Em dashes:** Do not use. Use commas or full stops.

**Exclamation marks:** Do not use.

**Approved terms (prefer these):**
- fabric composition names: "100% merino wool", "silk-linen blend", "cotton poplin"
- fit descriptors: "relaxed fit", "close to size", "size up for a looser fit"
- construction terms: "French seam", "bound hem", "fully lined"
- care instructions: "hand wash cold", "dry flat", "dry clean only"
- origin and sourcing: "woven in Italy", "made in Portugal", "Portuguese cotton"

**Banned terms:** "luxurious", "elevated", "curated", "stunning", "timeless", "classic" (as a standalone quality claim), "investment piece", "wardrobe essential", "season staple", "gorgeous", "amazing", "incredible", "perfect", "must-have".

**Competitor mentions:** Do not name competitors. Category language is acceptable: "comparable in price to Theory or Vince" is within bounds if sourced. "Unlike [brand name]" is not.

**Fit notes:** Always include at least one fit note in a product description. If the piece runs small, say so. If the model's measurements differ from the size shown, state both the size shown and the model's measurements.

**Stock and delivery claims:** Do not assert stock levels or delivery windows in generated copy unless the data is confirmed at the time of writing. "In stock" is acceptable as a live field. "Ships in 2-4 business days" is acceptable as a store policy statement. "Order before Friday" as a generated claim is not acceptable without confirmed dispatch capacity.
