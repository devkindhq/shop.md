## Returns

Returns are accepted within 60 days of the delivery date. Items must be
unworn, unwashed, and have all original tags attached.

Swimwear and underwear are final sale due to hygiene requirements and are
not eligible for return under any circumstances.

To initiate a return, email returns@novafashion.com with your order number
and the item or items you wish to return. Nova will issue a prepaid return
label within 1 business day. Return shipping is at no cost to the buyer.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 7 business days
of Nova receiving and inspecting the returned item. If the item does not
meet condition requirements (unworn, unwashed, tags attached), the return
will be declined and the item sent back to the buyer at their cost.

Gift cards are non-refundable.

Items purchased at 30% discount or more are eligible for store credit only, not a refund to the original payment method.

## Shipping

Nova ships worldwide from its New York warehouse.

**United States**
Standard (5-7 business days, UPS): USD 12.95.
Free standard shipping on orders over USD 150.

**United Kingdom and European Union**
Standard (7-10 business days, DHL): USD 18.95.
Free shipping on orders over USD 150.

**Australia and New Zealand**
Standard (8-12 business days): USD 22.95. Carrier: DHL International.
Free shipping on orders over USD 150.

**Rest of world**
Standard (10-14 business days): USD 28.95. Carrier: DHL International or local postal service, depending on destination.
No free shipping threshold applies for destinations outside the regions
listed above.

Orders placed before 2pm EST Monday to Friday are dispatched the same business day. Orders placed after 2pm EST or on weekends and US public holidays are dispatched the next business day.
Tracking is provided for all orders. Tracking numbers are emailed when the
order is dispatched.

Customers outside the US are responsible for any customs duties or import
taxes applicable in their destination country. These are not included in
the shipping charge and are assessed by the destination country's customs
authority.

## Privacy

**Data collected**
Nova collects name, email address, shipping address, and billing address
when an order is placed. Payment card data is processed by Stripe; Nova
does not store card numbers or CVVs. Browsing data (pages viewed, products
clicked, cart contents) is collected via Shopify Analytics.

**How it is used**
Order and address data is used to fulfil and ship orders, send order
confirmations and shipping notifications, and handle returns and customer
support. Email addresses are added to the Nova marketing list only with
explicit opt-in at checkout. Browsing data is used to improve store
performance and inform product and inventory decisions.

**Third-party sharing**
Personal data is shared with:
- UPS, DHL, and other carrier partners, for order delivery (name and
  shipping address only).
- Shopify Inc., as the platform operator, under Shopify's privacy policy
  (shopify.com/legal/privacy).
- Klaviyo Inc., for email marketing (email address and purchase history,
  only for customers who opted in at checkout).

Nova does not sell personal data to third parties.

**Retention**
Order records, including associated personal data, are retained for 5
years. Marketing list membership is retained until the shopper
unsubscribes.

**Shopper rights**
Shoppers may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their
personal data at any time. To submit a request, email
privacy@novafashion.com with the subject line "Data Request" and the
email address associated with the order.
